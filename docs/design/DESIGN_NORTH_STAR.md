# Design north star: Cave Light Atlas

The player is operating a digital cave exhibition, not browsing a dashboard. The map is the stage; side panels explain what the light means and the command dock turns understanding into action.

- Dark cave atmosphere with warm local exhibition surfaces.
- Cinnabar means immediate action, azure means discovery, malachite means repair, gold means connection and achievement.
- Hexagonal stone nodes, exhibition labels and instrument trays provide three distinct shape languages.
- Every visual effect explains a state change: focus, reachable route, damage, repair, contribution, event or result.
- Cultural artwork remains the primary iconography. Lucide is limited to generic controls.
