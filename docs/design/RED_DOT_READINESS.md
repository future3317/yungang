# Red Dot readiness

## Idea
Cave Light Atlas gives the product a memorable interaction metaphor: illuminating relationships between heritage sites.

## Form
A dark cave stage, stone nodes, exhibition labels and instrument trays establish a coherent visual grammar across landing, game and result states.

## Impact
The top bar states the shared goal; the map states place and risk; the dock states the next action; the context panels explain consequences before commitment.

## Function
Create, resume, focus, zoom, market selection, hand inspection, actions, pending choices and result states have explicit paths. Server state remains authoritative.

## Responsibility
Local font fallbacks remove runtime font dependence. Decorative art has empty alt text; meaningful scenes have descriptive alt text. No evidence is presented as research or expert review.

Evidence to collect before submission: five viewport screenshots, axe report, Lighthouse report, Playwright flow and human keyboard review.
