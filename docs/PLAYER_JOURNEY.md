# Player Journey

1. Choose a scenario, difficulty, player count, and optional seed on the landing page.
2. Read the forecast, then select an action from the active character's legal action dock.
3. Move only along available routes. Remote sites can be inspected but cannot be explored, repaired, or contributed to until the active character arrives.
4. Explore evidence, compare its domain and origin with the site task, then contribute qualifying cards.
5. Prepare for forecast events, survey or repair stressed routes, and progress projects and objectives.
6. End the round to resolve the event. The game state records the active phase, weathering pressure, effective rules, and outcome.
7. At victory or defeat, review the result screen, replay the exact seed, begin a fresh journey, or return home.
